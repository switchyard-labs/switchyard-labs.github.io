Operational boundaries

Real model-backed implementation, review and conflict-resolution runs were exercised on isolated local workers. The current Linode deployment serves the control plane and is not sized for routine Agent execution. These runs do not establish production throughput or distributed-worker scaling.

Conditional and unfinished certification

Worker Builds previews need a genuine scoped deployment connection. Browser credential issuance requires action-time approval; full authenticated live CLI dogfood needs a live sign-in. Firefox/WebKit were unavailable through the connected tooling. Final evidence and explicit limits are maintained in the source campaign ledger.
